## Products

Four tools, each with its own name, face and job. More are on the way.

| Product | What it does | Status | Website |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Endorphin** | AI-powered end-to-end testing. Write tests in plain English; AI runs them in a real browser and validates the result. | Available | [endorphinai.dev](https://endorphinai.dev/) |
| **Lomi** | Claude Code usage in the macOS menu bar: limits, sessions, subagents and cost. | Available | [lomi.software](https://lomi.software/) |
| **Linebacker** | An AI pull request reviewer. It finds bugs, risky input and security problems, and reports them by severity. | In development | [linebacker.software](https://linebacker.software/) |
| **ULU** | The AI agents beehive: a desktop app for Claude Code. Describe your project; it designs a team of AI agents and builds it. | In development | [aibeehive.software](https://aibeehive.software/) |

## Our philosophy

**We experiment with tomorrow's technology and build tools for today.**

An independent AI research and product studio. Exploring ideas, challenging conventions, and turning experiments into products.

- **Experiment without fear.** Explore unconventional ideas. Failure is part of discovery.
- **Build what matters.** Technology is valuable when it solves real problems, not when it demonstrates how sophisticated it is.
- **Make powerful technology accessible.** Give individuals and small teams capabilities that previously required significant resources.
